Hands on the pedal, not the mouse

Pair a Bluetooth foot pedal and it behaves exactly like the space bar. Need to fix a word mid-take? Press Edit, change it in place, and the stage updates instantly.

The dual-monitor setup

Two windows. One position.

Your control window stays on your screen with your scripts and settings. The stage window goes on a second display that faces the camera, mirrored or flipped for a teleprompter glass.

Mirror and Flip change only the stage. Your control view always stays readable, and both windows follow the exact same line, even when their sizes differ.

What is inside

These are real screenshots of the real app.

No mock-ups. Here is what you get the moment you press Start prompting.

Two monitors

Your screen on one side, the camera's on the other.

Run the control window on your own display and send the mirrored stage window to the second one. The stage preview in the control panel shows exactly what the camera reads, live, so you never have to look away.

Instant live edit

Fix a line mid-take. Every screen updates.

Hit Edit script, change a word, and the stage window and any paired device update as you type. No reload, no re-sending, no waiting between takes.

Live control

Pair a phone or tablet with one scan.

Scan the QR code and the script opens on that device in the right mode. Play and pause from either screen, and everything stays in sync. It is end-to-end encrypted, so your script is never readable on a server.

Why use it

Built for people who shoot scripts, not for people who browse apps.

Phone prompter apps are made for one screen and one person. A studio shoot needs a view for the operator, a view for the camera, and a way for the talent to keep their hands free. Space Prompter is built around exactly that.

Eyes stay on the lens

A bold guide line and a mirrored, flip-ready stage view mean you read where the camera is, not off to the side.

Perfect sync across screens

The control window and the stage window share one position down to the line, so you never lose your place when you adjust something.

Hands-free with a pedal

Any Bluetooth foot pedal works like the space bar. Tap to play and pause, double-tap to go back a paragraph, hold to jump to the start.

Fix a word without stopping

Press Edit, change the text right where it sits, and it saves and updates the stage preview on its own. No copy, paste and restart.

Private by default

There is no sign-up and no cloud account. Your scripts are saved in your own browser on your own device, and nowhere else. If you pair a phone, the connection is end-to-end encrypted, so the relay only passes along data it cannot read.

Open it, or install it

It is a single web page, so there is nothing to set up. Want it one tap away? Add it to your home screen or dock, and a small bar tells you when a new version is ready.

Pair your phone with a QR

Scan the code on your computer and your phone becomes a remote or a second screen. No account, no app to install, and the link is encrypted end to end.

Dim what you are not reading

Fade others keeps the current paragraph bright and softens the rest. You set how dim, so your eyes stay on the line you are about to say.

See it work in 10 seconds

Not ready to paste a script? Tap Demo text to drop in a sample, press Play to watch it scroll, then clear it with one tap.

Setups

One prompter, four ways to shoot.

Each setup has its own layout, so the controls never cover your script.

Best experience

Dual Monitor

The full studio rig. Control window on your screen, stage window on a second display facing the camera, with mirror and flip for a prompter glass and a live preview of what the camera sees.

Tablet

Big, touch-friendly text with plenty of room. Side panels open at half the screen and tuck away when you tap the script. A strong choice for a self-shot talking head.

Single Monitor

One screen, full width. The panels and controls fade away while you play and return when you move the mouse, so the script gets all of the space.

Mobile

Quick reads on a phone. Tap to pause and bring up the controls, tap again to hide everything and keep reading.

For the best experience, use Space Prompter with a dual-monitor setup or a tablet.
