About

Hi, I'm Shovon.

I'm a video creator and motion designer. Most of my days go to scripts, cameras and edits, and the rest to things I enjoy tinkering with: 3D modeling, interface design, WordPress plugins and automation on my Mac.

I build tools the way I shoot: for the job in front of me. If one of them saves you a take, that's the whole point.
